How ranking works. Company counts most, then interest, major and education. Exact matches beat related ones: a Statistics major scores partly on a Math search. When two brothers score the same, mentors come first, then alumni who updated their info through the chapter form, then grads from 2018 on, since they're the easiest to reach and know current recruiting.

These people are on the sigepcmu-alumni mailing list but not in the directory, so there's no job or major info yet. Names show only where the member set one on the list. List managers can post to everyone at once.

For SigEp brothers only. Contact info comes from the chapter's alumni directory and may be out of date, so check an address before you send. Please don't share this page or the emails outside the chapter.
